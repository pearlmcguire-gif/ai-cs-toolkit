# Weekly Leadership Digest: AI executive briefing

**The problem:** Leaders don't have time to read every account every week, and Customer Success leads don't have time to write a polished summary on top of their tracking.

**What it does:** The skill turns account health data (ideally Churn Radar's output) into a briefing leaders can read in two minutes: a snapshot, what needs their attention, workload concerns, wins, and the decisions needed from them.

## How to use it

1. Give the skill your current account health data, plus any wins or growth opportunities.
2. Run the skill in [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md).

## Sample run

[`sample-output/weekly-digest-2026-08-11.html`](sample-output/weekly-digest-2026-08-11.html) uses the same 15 made-up accounts as Churn Radar. The digest:

- Turns each critical account into a specific request, such as "15 minutes for an executive-to-executive call."
- Points out that one team member carries 3 of the 7 critical renewals, as a staffing question.
- Includes wins and growth opportunities alongside the risks.
- Ends with a list of decisions needed.

## Why it helps

- Leaders stay informed without reading a spreadsheet.
- Account data becomes decisions they can act on.
- Workload problems show up before a renewal slips.

*All data in this project is made up for demonstration.*
