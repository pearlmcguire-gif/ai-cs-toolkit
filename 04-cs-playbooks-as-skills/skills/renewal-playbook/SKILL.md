# Renewal Playbook Skill

## Purpose

Standardize how renewals are run so outcomes depend on preparation and process, not on which CSM happens to own the account. Based on general industry-standard renewal-management practice, not tied to any one company's specific process, and intended to be adapted to a real client's stage-gates and tooling.

## Timeline & Stage Gates

**120 days out: Health check.** Pull usage, support, and sentiment data (feed into the `churn-radar` skill if available). Confirm the renewal owner, the decision-maker(s), and whether procurement/legal will be involved this cycle.

**90 days out: Value recap & expansion scan.** Document what's been delivered since the last renewal against the original success criteria. Identify any legitimate expansion opportunity (more seats, new use case). Don't manufacture one if the account's usage doesn't support it.

**60 days out: Commercial proposal.** Draft pricing (renewal-only or renewal + expansion). Route internally for approval before it reaches the customer. Flag any account with a Yellow/Red health signal for extra review before proposing an increase.

**45 days out: Proposal delivered & stakeholder alignment.** Present to the economic buyer, not just the day-to-day contact. Confirm who else needs to sign off (legal, procurement, finance) and get ahead of their timelines.

**30 days out: Negotiation.** Address objections directly. If price is the stated objection but the underlying issue is adoption or trust, name that rather than defaulting to a discount.

**14 days out: Close or escalate.** If not signed, this is the point to involve leadership/exec sponsor rather than letting it slide to the contract's actual end date.

**Day 0, Renewed.** Log the outcome (on-time, late, discounted, expanded) and the reason, so the data feeds back into future health-scoring calibration.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate to leadership immediately, regardless of days-to-renewal, if: the account is Red/Critical on health score, the primary contact has left the company, or procurement introduces a competitive RFP.

## Output

A dated renewal plan for the account with the current stage, next action, owner, and an explicit flag if the account's health score suggests the renewal is at risk of anything other than an on-time, flat-or-up close.
