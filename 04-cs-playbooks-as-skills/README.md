# Playbooks as Skills: team know-how in a usable form

**The problem:** How to run a renewal, save an at-risk account or prepare a business review often lives in one experienced person's head or an old document. New hires take longer to ramp up, and the know-how leaves when that person does.

**What it does:** Three playbooks, each written as an AI skill a team member can use in the moment for step-by-step guidance:

- [`renewal-playbook`](skills/renewal-playbook/SKILL.md): a renewal process from 120 days out to close, with clear points to escalate.
- [`save-play-at-risk`](skills/save-play-at-risk/SKILL.md): finds out why an account is at risk before offering a discount.
- [`qbr-prep`](skills/qbr-prep/SKILL.md): a quarterly business review built around the customer's results, not a feature usage report.

## Why skills instead of a document

A playbook document gets read once and forgotten. A skill is used at the moment it's needed, for a specific renewal or account, and can draw on real account data, such as Churn Radar's output.

## About the content

These playbooks use widely known Customer Success practices, not any employer's own process. For a real team, they'd be adjusted to that team's stages and approvals.

## Why it helps

- Turns "ask the person who knows" into a written, teachable process.
- Gives every team member, new hires included, the same starting point for high-stakes moments.
- Keeps the team's know-how when people move on.
